DAGG-style IEP goal suggestions

Derived from the recommendation across Light's four communicative competencies. Edit before pasting into an IEP.

linguistic
  • Increase single-symbol vocabulary by 5 words per week.
  • Combine two symbols to make a request.
operational
  • Locate target buttons on a 7×12 grid with 80% accuracy.
  • Navigate from home board to a category folder and back independently.
social
  • Initiate at least 3 communicative turns per 10-minute interaction.
  • Respond to a partner question with at least 1 device-mediated turn within 10s.
strategic
  • Repair a communication breakdown using a second access method or symbol library.
  • Request help from a communication partner when an intended word is unavailable.

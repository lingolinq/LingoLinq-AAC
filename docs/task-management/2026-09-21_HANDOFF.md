## Measurement lessons (these caused real errors here)

1. **A probe that cannot fail is not evidence.** See the reload probe above.
2. **Never derive contrast from SCSS on a layered gradient.** `color-mix` chains and
   oklab interpolation do not compose the way an sRGB approximation says — a figure read
   6.40:1 where the rendered pixels were 4.78:1. Screenshot, decode the PNG (a ~25-line
   zlib+unfilter decoder; no PIL in this environment) and scan for the worst pixel.
3. **Any change to a SURFACE invalidates every colour chosen against it.** This bit three
   separate times in one afternoon. Re-measure after background changes, not only after
   colour changes.
4. **Exclude the element you are measuring.** Sampling `a[href$="/account"]` counted the
   pill itself; sampling a row's "background" caught the arrow glyph; sampling a card's
   "sheen" sampled the wrong corner. Each produced a confident wrong answer.
5. **Strip comments before brace-matching compiled CSS** — the build preserves `/* */`.
6. **Prove a lint-baseline shift before re-anchoring.** Same rule/column/severity AND the
   same line-order sequence, counts equal. Nearest-match heuristics give nonsense when a
   rule repeats at one column. Never regenerate.

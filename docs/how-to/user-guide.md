# LingoLinq how-to

Simple steps for everyday tasks. Start from the **Home** screen after you
sign in. Button names below match the English labels on screen.

---

## How to speak a short message

Use this when you want to tap a symbol and hear it spoken out loud.

1. From the Home screen, click **Continue Speaking**.
2. Click the symbol you want to say (for example, **Yes**).
3. Click **Speak sentence** (the microphone button in the bar at the top).

You should hear the word, and it stays in the message bar.

**If you don’t see Continue Speaking:** look for a **Speak Mode** card and
click that instead. Some Home screens show **Let’s Communicate** or
**Open My Communication Board** — those open your board the same way.

**If a different board opens:** that’s okay. Tap any symbol on the board
you see, then click **Speak sentence**.

---

## How to create a board

Use this when you want a new, empty communication board.

1. From the Home screen, click **Create a Board**.
2. Click **Create My Own Board**.
3. Type a name in the **Name** box (for example, “My Board”).
4. Click **Create Board**.

You should land on your new board. A blank board says it hasn’t been set
up yet, and you’ll see **Edit this Board**. You can leave rows, columns,
and **Description** as they are.

## How to change a preference (Start in Speak Mode)

Use this when you want LingoLinq to open in Speak Mode as soon as you
launch the app — or to turn that off.

1. From the Home screen, click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top).
2. In the **Basics** section, check or uncheck **Start app in Speak Mode on launch**.
3. Click **Save Preferences**.

**Basics** is already open when Settings loads. After you save, the app
leaves the Settings page. Open **Settings** again if you want to double-check
that the box stayed as you left it.

---

## How to require a PIN when exiting Speak Mode

Use this when you want a PIN before anyone can leave Speak Mode (for example,
to keep a communicator focused on their board).

1. From the Home screen, click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top).
2. In the **Basics** section, check **Require a PIN when exiting Speak Mode**.
3. Type a 4-digit PIN in **Speak Mode PIN**.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

The next time someone tries to leave Speak Mode, LingoLinq will ask for that
PIN.

**Optional:** you can also check **Don't show hint for discovering forgotten
PIN**, but only if you are sure you will remember the PIN or can look it up
on another device.

## How to turn on High Contrast mode

Use this when button pictures are hard to see and you want a bolder,
easier-to-read look.

1. From the Home screen, click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top).
2. Click **Styling** to open that section (it starts collapsed).
3. Under **High Contrast**, check **Show all button images in high-contrast mode**.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

Your board symbols should now use high-contrast images.

---

## How to change the app's language

Use this when you want menus, buttons, and on-screen text in a different
language.

1. From the Home screen, click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top).
2. In the **Basics** section, open the **Preferred Language** menu and choose
   your language (for example, **English (default)** or another language in
   the list).
3. Click **Save Preferences**.

After you save, the app reloads with your chosen language. Open **Settings**
again if you want to confirm your choice stuck.

**Note:** this changes the app interface language. To switch the language on
a specific board while speaking, use **Options** → **Switch Language** on
that board instead.

---

## How to turn on Auto-Capitalize

Use this when you want the first word of each new sentence in the message bar
to start with a capital letter automatically.

1. From the Home screen, click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top).
2. Click **Startup and Extras** to open that section (it starts collapsed).
3. Under **Auto-Capitalize**, check **Automatically capitalize new sentences in the vocalization box**.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

---

## How to adjust notification email frequency

Use this when you want more or fewer email summaries about communicator
activity (or none at all).

1. From the Home screen, click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top).
2. Click **Notifications** to open that section (it starts collapsed).
3. Under **Usage Summaries**, open the menu and pick how often you want
   emails:
   - **Don't Email Me Communicator Reports**
   - **Email Me Weekly Communicator Reports**
   - **Email Me Communicator Reports Every Two Weeks**
   - **Email Me Monthly Communicator Reports**
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

The **Notifications** section also has **Goal Updates** and **Share
Notifications** if you want to fine-tune other alerts later.

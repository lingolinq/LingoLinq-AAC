Export CoughDrop boards

  1. Log in to CoughDrop and open your home board.
  2. Click the bookmark you saved.
  3. Wait for the JSON file to download, then import it in LingoLinq.

If your browser adds unsafe: at the start of the bookmark address, delete that prefix before saving. Dragging from this page usually avoids that.
